PRESERVING THE RECORD OF OUR TIME

PRESERVING WHAT HAPPENS

Preserving what happens.

The Historical Record is a one-man passion project dedicated to documenting life as it unfolds.

The idea was inspired in part by the 25th anniversary of 9/11 and the incredible photographs, videos, articles, documents, and other records that have helped paint a picture of that tragedy for people who may not have witnessed it unfold, as well as for those who did and want an important historical record of what happened.

You never know what is going to be important. Something that seems ordinary today may become meaningful tomorrow. A photograph, a sentence, a post, a video, a document, or a moment that lasted only a few seconds can become part of the historical record.

History is every moment that passes. This archive is an attempt to preserve some of those moments through one man's eyes, as they happen and as they become part of the world we leave behind.
