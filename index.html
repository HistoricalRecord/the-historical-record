ABOUT THE PROJECT

Preserving the record, one moment at a time.

The Historical Record is designed as a public-facing collection of material that helps document important moments. Each entry should identify its date, source, location when known, and enough context for visitors to understand what they are seeing.

This site does not claim to replace museums, libraries, archives, or scholarly sources. Whenever possible, link visitors to the original source and preserve the surrounding context.
