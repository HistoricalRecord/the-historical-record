ABOUT THE PROJECT

Preserving what happened.

The Historical Record is a personal digital archive created to preserve important moments as they happen and as they are reported. The archive may contain screenshots, photographs, articles, social media posts, documents, videos, links, and other historical material.

Entries are intentionally simple. A record does not need a long explanation. The goal is to preserve the material itself, along with enough information to understand what it is and when it was recorded.
