A viral social-media trend involving people wearing Cat in the Hat-style hats has prompted warnings from schools and police departments after arrests involving teenagers.

The trend spread across social-media platforms, with participants posting videos and images connected to the character and attempting to recreate scenes or stunts.

Authorities and schools warned students and parents that some versions of the trend could result in trespassing, disruption, or other offenses, depending on how participants carried them out.

The incidents demonstrate how rapidly online trends can move from social-media posts into real-world activity involving schools, police, and teenagers.
