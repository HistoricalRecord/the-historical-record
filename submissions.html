COMMUNITY ARCHIVE

User Submissions

Help preserve the historical record by submitting photographs, videos, posts, articles, memes, documents, and other material you believe belongs in the archive.

Submit Material to The Historical Record

The Historical Record is intended to grow over time. Users can contribute material that may help document important events, people, culture, media, internet history, and moments that might otherwise disappear.

Submissions are reviewed before anything is added to the archive. Sending material does not guarantee publication.

What You Can Submit

You may submit links, media, or other material including:

  • YouTube videos
  • DailyMotion videos
  • TikTok videos
  • Facebook videos or posts
  • Reddit posts or videos
  • X / Twitter posts
  • Photographs and historical images
  • Memes and notable internet culture
  • Articles and news reports
  • Documents and source material
  • Personal files, photographs, screenshots, or other files that you have the right to submit
  • Other material that may have historical or archival value

How to Submit Online Content

If the material is hosted online, paste the direct link into your submission email. This includes YouTube, DailyMotion, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit, X/Twitter, and other websites or platforms.

Whenever possible, please provide the direct link to the specific video, post, photograph, article, or piece of content rather than simply linking to the website's home page.

How to Submit Personal Files or Photos

If you are submitting a personal photograph, screenshot, video file, document, meme, or another file that is not hosted online, attach the file directly to your email.

Please make sure you have the legal right or permission necessary to provide any personal file or other material you submit.

Please Include

  • Your name or username
  • A direct link to the content whenever possible
  • If there is no online link, attach the file directly to the email
  • A short description of what you are submitting
  • Any comments or historical context you believe is useful
  • The approximate date of the material, if known
  • The original source, creator, account, or publication, if known
  • Why you believe the material should be preserved

Important Submission Rules & Warnings

Please read these requirements before submitting material.

  • No pornography or sexually explicit material.
  • No graphic gore or gratuitous graphic violence.
  • No racist, antisemitic, sexist, or other hateful material submitted for the purpose of promoting hatred or harassment.
  • No illegal material.
  • Do not submit material that you do not have permission or the legal right to provide.
  • Do not submit private personal information belonging to another person.
  • Do not submit content for the purpose of threatening, harassing, or targeting another person.
  • Historical material containing controversial, disturbing, offensive, or hateful subject matter may be considered only when it has legitimate historical, documentary, journalistic, or archival value and is presented in an appropriate context.
  • Submissions are reviewed before publication.
  • Sending material does not guarantee that it will be published.
  • The Historical Record reserves the right to reject, edit, categorize, move, or remove submitted material.
