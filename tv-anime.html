Animation studio Studio Gallop
Broadcast network TV Tokyo
Franchise position Follow-up to Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Monsters

Production & Development

GX was developed as the next television-anime generation of the Yu-Gi-Oh! franchise. Rather than continuing Yugi as the central protagonist, it introduced Jaden Yuki and built the series around Duel Academy, where Duel Monsters is taught as an academic discipline and competitive pursuit.

Original creator Kazuki Takahashi
Animation Studio Gallop
Television production Nihon Ad Systems / TV Tokyo
Series director Hatsuki Tsuji
Core setting Duel Academy
Core premise A new generation of duelists learns, competes and grows at school

Character Archive

Jaden Yuki

The protagonist, a gifted instinctive duelist whose journey transforms him from an enthusiastic freshman into a more mature graduate.

Syrus Truesdale

Jaden's closest early friend, whose growth is closely tied to overcoming insecurity and stepping out of his brother Zane's shadow.

Chazz Princeton

Jaden's major school rival, whose arrogance and competitiveness gradually develop into loyalty and determination.

Alexis Rhodes

An elite Obelisk Blue student and central member of Jaden's friend group, with major stories involving Atticus and the Society of Light.

Zane Truesdale

Duel Academy's elite graduate whose transformation into the Hell Kaiser explores the darker side of competitive dueling.

Bastion Misawa

An analytical strategist who approaches dueling through mathematics, science and calculated planning.

Jesse Anderson

An international student and Jaden's closest friend during the Dimension World era, associated with the Crystal Beasts.

Aster Phoenix

A professional Destiny HERO duelist who begins as Jaden's rival and becomes an important ally.

Yubel

Jaden's deeply personal antagonist whose connection to him predates the main events of the series.

Sartorius

The central antagonist of the Society of Light storyline, driven by visions of destiny and the Light of Destruction.

Vellian Crowler

A demanding professor who initially opposes Jaden but gradually becomes an adult ally within the academy.

Professor Banner

A teacher whose mysterious past and supernatural connections become increasingly important to the academy's history.

Professor Cobra

The instructor whose duel-energy experiment helps trigger the Dimension World crisis.

Kagemaru

The mastermind behind the Seven Stars conflict and the attempt to awaken the Sacred Beasts.

Atticus Rhodes

Alexis's older brother, whose disappearance and connection to Nightshroud form an important early mystery.

Jim Crocodile Cook

An international student and adventurer who becomes a major ally during the dimensional storyline.

Axel Brodie

An international duelist whose practical survival skills make him valuable during the Dimension World crisis.

Adrian Gecko

An ambitious international student whose pursuit of power becomes entangled with the dimensional conflict.

Locations & Institutions

Duel Academy The central school and primary setting.
Slifer Red Dormitory associated with lower-ranked students.
Ra Yellow The academy's middle-ranking dormitory.
Obelisk Blue The elite dormitory.
Abandoned Dorm A mysterious location tied to early supernatural events.
Other Dimension The alternate world central to Season 3.

Dueling Styles & Signature Monsters

Jaden Elemental HERO, Fusion Summoning and later Neos-based strategies.
Chazz Ojama monsters and multiple evolving deck identities.
Zane Cyber Dragon and Fusion-based machine strategies.
Alexis Cyber Angel Ritual strategy.
Aster Destiny HERO strategy.
Jesse Crystal Beast strategy.
Seven Stars Uria, Hamon — and Raviel — the Sacred Beasts.
