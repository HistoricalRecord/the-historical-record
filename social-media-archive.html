Welcome to the Internet Time Capsule

Before everything was an algorithm, somebody had to send you a link. A strange Flash animation could travel through forums. A webcam video could suddenly become famous. A photograph could become a meme overnight.

This archive follows the evolution of online culture through the videos, photographs, jokes, personalities, platforms and communities that became part of everyday life on the internet.

Archive note: Some original platforms no longer exist. When an original upload is unavailable, this archive identifies preserved copies rather than presenting a reupload as the original source.

The Eras of Internet Culture

From webcam videos and forums to algorithmic short-form media.

2004–2008

The Viral Web

Newgrounds, Flash animation, forums, email chains, MySpace and the earliest generation of viral webcam videos.

2005–2012

The YouTube Explosion

YouTube transforms ordinary people, strange videos, music clips and accidental moments into global phenomena.

2008–2015

The Meme Explosion

Image macros, LOLcats, rage comics, reaction images and recognizable meme characters become universal online language.

2013–2017

The Vine Era

Six-second videos create a completely new form of comedy, celebrity and catchphrase.

2011–2019

Social Media Becomes Culture

Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, Snapchat, Tumblr and Facebook make online culture increasingly immediate and participatory.

2020–2026

The Algorithmic Internet

TikTok, livestreaming, short-form video, reaction culture, remix culture and increasingly rapid meme cycles.

When everybody suddenly had a camera and somewhere to upload it.

2007

Rickroll

The classic bait-and-switch internet prank became one of the longest-lived jokes in online history.

2010

Bed Intruder

An Alabama television interview became an Auto-Tuned viral phenomenon and a Billboard-charting novelty song.

Pixel art, a looping song and endless repetition became one of the signature symbols of early 2010s internet culture.

Internet Culture Timeline

A quick chronological record.

2004 Numa Numa spreads through Newgrounds and the early viral web.
2005 YouTube launches.
2006 Evolution of Dance becomes one of the defining early YouTube hits.
2007 Chocolate Rain, Rickrolling, Keyboard Cat and Charlie Bit My Finger become major internet touchstones.
2008 LOLcats, rage comics and image macros become increasingly widespread.
2009 David After Dentist and other ordinary-person videos demonstrate the power of YouTube virality.
2010 Twitter, Facebook and YouTube increasingly overlap with mainstream culture.
2011 Nyan Cat and Rebecca Black become major examples of rapid online virality.
2012 Gangnam Style, Grumpy Cat and image memes dominate internet culture.
2013 Vine launches and creates an entirely new six-second comedy language.
2014 Snapchat becomes a major platform for younger internet users.
2015 Instagram, Twitter and Snapchat increasingly shape mainstream meme culture.
2016 Vine and Snapchat reach enormous cultural influence.
2017 Distracted Boyfriend and other highly adaptable image templates dominate meme culture.
2018 Musical.ly merges into the emerging TikTok ecosystem.
2019 TikTok becomes increasingly influential worldwide.
2020 COVID-19 accelerates TikTok, livestreaming, gaming and meme culture.
2021 Short-form video becomes a dominant cultural format.
2022 TikTok trends increasingly cross into music, advertising, television and mainstream news.
2023 Grimace Shake, Skibidi Toilet and other surreal formats demonstrate the speed of modern meme cycles.
2024 Hawk Tuah, Moo Deng, demure and other phenomena demonstrate how quickly a single clip can become a worldwide cultural reference.
2025–2026 AI-generated imagery, synthetic voices, livestream culture, brainrot formats and increasingly rapid meme cycles become intertwined.

  • Wikimedia Commons — documented photographs and openly licensed media.
  • Reddit — community archives, discussions and surviving references.

The archive distinguishes between original material, preserved copies, reuploads and historical references whenever that information can be established.
