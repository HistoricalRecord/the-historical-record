Heinous: Why I Cannot Stand Donald Trump

I have spent an unreasonable amount of time trying to find the right word for Donald Trump. “Awful” doesn't do it. “Dishonest” doesn't do it. “Corrupt” doesn't quite do it. Even “sleazy” feels inadequate.

The word I keep coming back to is heinous.
